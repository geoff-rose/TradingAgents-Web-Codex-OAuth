Swing Trade

Simulated swing trading on the paper IBKR account — fitted range-model proposals you approve, entry/target refreshed daily, tracked through to fill and P&L.

Trade Size & Model Percentiles

Trade size applies to the next proposal scan, not retroactively. Entry/target percentiles control the range model (phase-3 §6): entry_q is how deep a pullback to wait for (lower = rarer, deeper dips only); target_q is how far a rally to aim for. Expect these to need refinement — the defaults are from one backtest sweep on a small out-of-sample sample, not a final answer.
