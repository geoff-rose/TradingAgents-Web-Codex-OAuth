Watchlist

Tickers pinned from the scanner or the announcement feed, with the day's prices and a one-click hand-off into the paper-trade log. Adding something here records nothing and risks nothing — it is a list of things to look at, kept apart from the trade log on purpose so it can never distort that log's P&L.

Prices are delayed — they come from the same daily bars the scanner reads, so the two pages can never disagree with each other. Last is the most recent trade, not a live quote. The quote source is a single swappable function (watchlist.get_quotes), so pointing this at IBKR for live prices later changes nothing above it.

Marked to market from daily closes. Nothing closes automatically - the exit is the decision you are trying to learn, so the page will flag a breached stop or an elapsed hold and leave it to you. Expect a median near zero with a few large winners: the measured payoff was 46% positive with a 99th percentile of +88%, so a losing streak is the normal shape here, not evidence it is broken.

Automated paper books

Two timer-driven books, kept separate on purpose - they are different hypotheses and pooling them destroys both records. Overnight buys the 15:45 volume candidates at the close and exits at the next open. Gap reversion buys frozen-cohort names that gapped down >3%, entered mid-morning (not at the open - that price is not tradeable on this signal) and exited at the same day's close.
