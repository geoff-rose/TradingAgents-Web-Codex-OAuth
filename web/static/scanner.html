What this looks for

A move of +8% or more on 3x normal volume, with an announcement to explain it. Those thresholds are measured, not chosen: below +8% volume carried no continuation information, and the forward-return gradient only separates from the no-news bucket at 3x and above. The historical edge showed up over 3-10 days, never next-day - every volume bucket was negative at 1 day.

Both percentages are measured from the previous close, not from the open. Move % is last vs prev close — the whole move. Gap % is the open vs prev close — the part that was already there at the opening auction, before any intraday trading. Move % vs open is last vs the open — what is left once the gap has already happened, and the live version of the Open→Close % in the gap-outcomes table below. So a row with Move 12%, Gap 11% and Move-vs-open ~0% barely budged once the market opened; one with Move 12%, Gap 0% and Move-vs-open 12% did all of it during the session. Prev close, Open and Last are all shown so the arithmetic is checkable.

Nothing here is proven tradeable. Where the effect was strongest (under 50c) the round-trip spread exceeded it; in the one band where costs were low enough (50c-$2) it was not statistically distinguishable from buying any mover. Rows are flagged by price band so the uneconomic ones stay visible rather than hidden. This page exists so you can watch it live and form your own view - and unlike every backtest here, a live scan has no survivorship bias.

End-of-day volume — overnight hold candidates

Names trading well above their normal volume for this point in the session. Measured over 2 years of hourly bars on the top 500: buying the closing auction and selling the next opening auction returned ~+0.19% per night unconditionally, and ~+0.34% when the stock ran >6x normal volume — 8 of 9 quarters positive, and not driven by a few outliers.

The exit is the next open, not later. Selling at 11:00 instead gives back 69% of the gain, and the win rate falls to a coin flip. Two auction fills, nothing between.

The ratio compares today's volume so far against the same hours of the previous 20 sessions, so it means the same thing whenever you look — but only after 15:00 is it the statistic the strategy was actually measured on. Before then it is a live preview. +0.34% per night is small: a daily round trip has to cost less than that, and ~21% of these historically do not move overnight at all.

Gap outcomes — does the move hold past the open?

Every mover the scanner has ever flagged is logged with its previous close, open, high and close. A stock qualifies here on its move vs the previous close — this answers whether it then actually held above its open by the end of the day, or gapped up and drifted down giving the move back. Only finalized days count (confirmed after the close by a 16:15 Sydney timer, not "last traded so far" mid-session).

News is how many announcements that company released for that session (⚡ = at least one ASX price-sensitive flag), and AI score is the same 0-100 score the ASX dashboard gave them — the net score across all of that company's announcements for the session where one was computed, otherwise its most material single announcement. Both are snapshotted at scan time, so they stay readable long after the announcement feed has rolled off. A dash means no record; none means we looked and there was no announcement.
