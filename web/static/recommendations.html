Morning candidates

A pre-open, point-in-time ranking for ASX names that could be held for a few sessions. It rebuilds the candidate list from fresh ASX announcements, verified AI scores, overnight markets, VIX, rates, FX, Asian markets, commodities, sector beta, completed technicals and tradability. The score is a research ranking, not a probability of profit; the page can abstain when the evidence is weak. Testing treats previous close → session close as the primary full-day result, with open → close shown separately as the executable entry lens.

What this uses

Technical: completed daily OHLCV only, never today's partial bar; recent full-session momentum, trend confirmation, extension and volatility guards are causal at generation time. Universe: recommendation candidates are capped at the live top-500 market-cap band, with minimum history and median dollar-turnover checks. News: current ASX releases, verified AI scores, and a clearly labelled headline-only proxy while document scoring is pending. Fresh context: SPI/US futures, VIX, US 10Y, AUD/USD, Asian markets, gold, silver, copper, oil, iron ore when valid, and local sector beta. Catalyst continuation: scored price-sensitive catalysts can remain eligible for five subsequent ASX sessions, but only with above-50d, positive recent relative strength and at least two technical confirmations; decay and older evidence remain visible. Rotation: recent picks are excluded for three sessions unless they have a new current-session catalyst. Testing: prior-close-to-close is the primary recommendation-session result; open-to-close and later forward returns remain visible separately.
