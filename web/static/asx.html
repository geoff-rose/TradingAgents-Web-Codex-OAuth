What is moving now. The cash indices above are frozen from 06:00 Sydney until the US opens at 23:30, so through the whole ASX session they say nothing about the present; these trade nearly around the clock. Each change is measured against the contract's own previous settlement, never a cash close — the two sit at different levels and mixing them reports a basis, not an overnight move.
